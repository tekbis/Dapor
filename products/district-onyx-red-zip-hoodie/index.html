4. How to measure

Use a soft tape · Keep it level · Do not pull tight
Chest
Around the fullest part of the chest, under the arms. Keep the tape relaxed.
Waist
Around the natural waist where the garment will sit. Do not measure over bulky clothing.
Hip / seat
Around the fullest part of the hips and seat, with feet together.
Inseam
From the crotch seam to the desired hem along the inner leg.
Outseam
From the top of the waistband to the hem along the outside leg.
Chest width
Lay the garment flat and measure pit to pit. Double for circumference.
